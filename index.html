EPL Tactics Board

Seven views of how each club builds, presses, shapes up and attacks set pieces, and a Matchup tab that pits any club's build-up against any other club's pressing.

Open board

Every number on the board comes from real events, and every sequence is modelled rather than replayed. Passing chains and press episodes are stored as tries of real transitions with low-support branches pruned, so "most likely" walks the tree deterministically and "one real draw" samples it. Small-sample caveats sit in the captions, not the footnotes.

  • Passing. Build-up by zone and channel, progressive outlets vs recycling anchors, sequence lengths, long-ball share.
  • Pressing. Press episodes from possession spells: PPDA by third, block height, regain speed, coordination, intensity by game state.
  • Formations. Real Opta formation events and in-game shape changes, not shapes inferred from average positions.
  • Set Pieces. Corners and free kicks plotted at real delivery and shot coordinates, with takers and first-contact defenders.
  • Players. Full squad profiles, player-vs-player duels resolved through Opta's linked-event qualifiers, minutes and starts.
  • Matchup. Attacker's build-up share × defender's regain multiplier per zone, ranked Exploit / Contested / Danger, with a stated approach.

Opposition Dossiers

One command turns every tactical layer into a standardised pre-match dossier per club, published as an HTML board and a Markdown file each.

Open dossiers

Each dossier opens with an evidence-based plan to beat the club, then the triggers and patterns a coach wants on one page: what sets off their press, what they do when ahead and when behind, formation changes by score state with the typical minute, substitution timing and the most-used subs, and the pressing linchpins.

  • Style. Possession, tilt, directness, crosses, deep completions, shot quality, set-piece and counter shares, corridors, entries by channel.
  • Defending. PPDA overall and by third, block height, high recoveries, regain speed, aerials, errors, and the channel opponents enter through.
  • Game states. Tilt, PPDA, shots, counters and crosses when level, leading and trailing; first-goal effects.
  • People. Danger men, creators, key players by involvement, current absentees, last season's usual XI with departures flagged.

Model that keeps its own score · EPL + Champions League 2026/27

Match Forecasting

A state-space Dixon-Coles rating model with injury and fatigue layers and minute-level simulation, run unattended on a schedule and graded against results and the closing market.

Team ratings are per-season nodes with club-specific carry-over, a squad-value prior and hierarchical shrinkage across league groups. Injuries scrape from Transfermarkt into per-player multipliers, fatigue reads from fixture congestion, and each match is simulated minute by minute. Every run logs its pre-kick-off numbers; once results land, the last snapshot before kick-off is scored.

The point of the live record is that it is allowed to look bad. A backtest fit blind to a full season showed the market's closing prices ahead of the raw model, and the live record so far says the same, so the model is used as one input beside the market rather than instead of it.

Working principle

Never show a coach more precision than the data supports.

Event data without tracking tells you what happened on the ball. It does not tell you where the other twenty players stood. Every choice in these tools follows from that limit.

  • Positional estimates for off-ball players are tiered as interpolated, recent or estimated, and labelled as such on the pitch.
  • Formations come from Opta's own formation events. Inferring shape from average positions was tried and dropped.
  • Indirect free kicks are not linked to a resulting shot, because no qualifier connects them and a time-window guess would be a fabrication.
  • Two upstream data faults were caught and handled before reaching a report: player ids shared by two people at different clubs, and mirrored aerial duels that produced impossible 100% win rates when thresholded per side.
  • Backtests and the live record are published even where they favour the market.

Stack

Data

Opta-schema event feeds, Understat, StatsBomb open data, FBref, Transfermarkt, UEFA and Premier League fixture APIs. Bronze raw JSON, silver star schema in DuckDB and Parquet, analysis in SQL and pandas.

Modelling

Python with pandas, NumPy and scikit-learn. Bootstrapped transition tries, logistic xG, state-space Dixon-Coles with hierarchical shrinkage, Monte Carlo season and minute-level match simulation.

Delivery

Single-file interactive HTML with SVG pitch graphics, Markdown dossiers, scheduled unattended runs with logged and self-scored predictions. Professionally: SQL on Postgres, Hex apps, AWS, Docker, Grafana.
