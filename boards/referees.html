Referee profiles

How to play each referee, once you allow for the teams he gets: how much he lets go, how fast he books, what a tactical foul costs against him and who on your side is at risk. Read it with two honest limits. 48 of our 71 officials have fewer than 8 matches and cannot be judged at all, and the referee is the small term: swapping one Premier League club for another moves a match by about 7.7 free kicks, swapping the most lenient official for the strictest by about 2.9.
