Australia Football Board

Eleven Socceroos and Matildas matches from the 2022 and 2023 World Cups, built on StatsBomb 360 — event data plus the measured position of every player the broadcast camera could see. The club boards in this portfolio have no positional data at all. This one does, so it is used for two things: to show the Australian sides' shape as it actually was, and to put an error bar on every positional estimate the other boards make.
